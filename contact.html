Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my data bundle?

We deliver data bundles within 2-5 minutes of payment confirmation. In rare cases, it may take up to 10 minutes during peak hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mobile Money (MoMo), Bank Transfer, and CashApp payments. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Do you provide data for all networks?

Yes, we provide data bundles for MTN, Vodafone, and AirtelTigo networks in Ghana.

What if I don't receive my data bundle?

If you don't receive your data bundle within 10 minutes, please contact our support team immediately. We'll investigate and resolve the issue or provide a refund.

Are your prices competitive?

Yes, we offer the most competitive prices in the market with no hidden charges or extra fees.

How can I track my order?

You'll receive a confirmation message via WhatsApp or Telegram once your order is processed and delivered.
